# Forecasting Model Development: Al-Powered Energy Prediction

Introduction to the Problem:​

1. In the energy sector, forecasting load accurately is crucial for grid stability and efficiency.​
2. Traditional methods often struggle with non-stationarity, complex patterns, and real-world data imperfections.   ​
 

Consequences of Poor Forecasting:
​
1. Operational inefficiencies and increased costs.​
2. Risk of overloading or underutilizing grid resources.​
3. Negative impact on renewable energy integration.​
 

Limitations of Current Approaches:
​
1. Over-reliance on single-model systems.​
2. Lack of explainability and robustness.​
3. Limited cross-dataset generalization.​
 

Client Details
​
The ARC Research Hub for Integrated Energy Storage Solutions is a nationally recognised research centre focused on developing advanced energy storage technologies. It collaborates with leading industry partners and academic institutions to deliver innovative solutions supporting Australia's renewable energy transition.​
​

Project Requirement:
​
1. Develop a prototype forecasting platform incorporating multiple ML/DL models.​
2. Integrate evaluation matrix for model comparison.​
3. Consider how to improve the Interpretability.
​

Key Tasks:​

1. Data Collection & Preparation: Obtain, clean, and structure time-series data.​
2. Model Development: Build baseline and advanced forecasting models.​
3. Model Improvement: Use some Interpretability methods​
4. Validation & Reporting: Evaluate models and document insights.​
 

Inclusions​

1. Focus on time-series load/PV forecasting.​
2. Use of at least two types of DL/ML models.​
3. Feature Engineering.​
4. Metrics: MAE, RMSE, MAPE.​
5. Platform Development using some online platforms.​
​

Exclusions​

1. Handling confidential or sensitive energy market data.​
2. Non-energy related datasets.​
3. Large-scale distributed computing setup (e.g., Kubernetes clusters).
4. Project members will/should acquire the following skills and knowledge:​
5. Foundational level of understanding in Machine Learning/Deep Learning Algorithms​
6. Foundational level of understanding in Software engineering and Development.​
7. Foundational level of understanding in project management and Software Development Lifecycle​
8. Intermediate level of written and verbal communication in a professional setting​
​

Recommended Tools/Technologies
​
- Language: Python​
- Libraries: scikit-learn, XGBoost, TensorFlow/Keras, Prophet​
- Source Code Management: GitHub​
- Development Environment: Colab, VS Code, or Jupyter Notebook
