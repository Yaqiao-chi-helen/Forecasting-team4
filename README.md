# Forecasting Model Development: Al-Powered Energy Prediction

Introduction to the Problem:​
In the energy sector, forecasting load accurately is crucial for grid stability and efficiency.​
Traditional methods often struggle with non-stationarity, complex patterns, and real-world data imperfections.   ​
 

Consequences of Poor Forecasting:​
Operational inefficiencies and increased costs.​
Risk of overloading or underutilizing grid resources.​
Negative impact on renewable energy integration.​
 

Limitations of Current Approaches:​
Over-reliance on single-model systems.​
Lack of explainability and robustness.​
Limited cross-dataset generalization.​
 

Client Details​
The ARC Research Hub for Integrated Energy Storage Solutions is a nationally recognised research centre focused on developing advanced energy storage technologies. It collaborates with leading industry partners and academic institutions to deliver innovative solutions supporting Australia's renewable energy transition.​
​

Project Requirement:​
Develop a prototype forecasting platform incorporating multiple ML/DL models.​
Integrate evaluation matrix for model comparison.​
Consider how to improve the Interpretability.
​

Key Tasks:​
Data Collection & Preparation: Obtain, clean, and structure time-series data.​
Model Development: Build baseline and advanced forecasting models.​
Model Improvement: Use some Interpretability methods​
Validation & Reporting: Evaluate models and document insights.​
 

Inclusions​
Focus on time-series load/PV forecasting.​
Use of at least two types of DL/ML models.​
Feature Engineering.​
Metrics: MAE, RMSE, MAPE.​
Platform Development using some online platforms.​
​

Exclusions​
Handling confidential or sensitive energy market data.​
Non-energy related datasets.​
Large-scale distributed computing setup (e.g., Kubernetes clusters).
Project members will/should acquire the following skills and knowledge:​
Foundational level of understanding in Machine Learning/Deep Learning Algorithms​
Foundational level of understanding in Software engineering and Development.​
Foundational level of understanding in project management and Software Development Lifecycle​
Intermediate level of written and verbal communication in a professional setting​
​

Recommended Tools/Technologies​
Language: Python​

Source Code Management: GitHub​
